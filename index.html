How I structure engineering work

One continuous R&D loop, not isolated software skills.

This is the project workflow I use to organize work: observe the asset, define the problem, build a model, analyze it, integrate electronics, then validate against field constraints.

1

Survey

Machine identity, operating environment, access, electrical/service areas.

2

Requirements

Turn field observations into measurable design and monitoring constraints.

3

CAD

Build or handle geometry, assemblies and packaging in Siemens NX.

4

CAE

Evaluate stress, deformation and thermal behaviour in ANSYS.

5

Electronics

Sensor/controller logic, simulated wiring, OBD/CAN and signal flow.

6

Validate

Compare the design against real installation, vibration, dust and serviceability.
