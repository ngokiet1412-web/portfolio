QUESTION TO ENGINEERING RESPONSE.

Simulation is used to compare defined conditions and guide design decisions — not as a substitute for physical validation.

EVALUATION DETAILS

ASSUMPTIONS MADE VISIBLE

These focused crops come from the same ANSYS capture. They illustrate the sequence from a bounded question to an interpretable output.

01 / QUESTION

Define what the model must answer.

Choose a specific structural, thermal or vibration question. The geometry, simplifications and outputs should all serve that question.

  • State the comparison or failure mode of interest
  • Record geometry and material assumptions
  • Choose outputs that support a decision
02 / SETUP

Build a reviewable simulation setup.

Materials, contacts, supports, loads and mesh strategy are treated as engineering inputs. Each one must be traceable before the result is interpreted.

  • Check contacts and boundary conditions
  • Refine mesh where the question requires it
  • Review units, load paths and solver messages
03 / INTERPRET

Read patterns, not only maxima.

Contours help identify where the design responds strongly under the modeled conditions. Results are reviewed against assumptions and used to propose the next design iteration.

  • Inspect deformation and stress distribution
  • Check whether peaks are physical or numerical
  • Carry uncertainty into the design decision
Evidence boundary

A simulation result is evidence for the defined model and assumptions. Physical validation remains a separate engineering step.
