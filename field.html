SURVEY DETAILS

CONTEXT TO DESIGN CONSTRAINT

Six real field frames move from the whole machine and worksite toward detailed component interfaces.

01 / MACHINE CONTEXT

Identify the asset and its working envelope.

Whole-machine views establish scale, major systems and the space available around the equipment.

  • Confirm visible machine configuration
  • Note working envelope and nearby structures
  • Locate likely service and installation zones
02 / OPERATING ENVIRONMENT

Read the environment around the machine.

Terrain, dirt, water, heat, vibration and access conditions influence enclosure, mounting, routing and maintenance choices.

  • Observe exposure and contamination
  • Check safe access for inspection
  • Record environmental constraints for design
03 / INTERFACE INSPECTION

Move from visible detail to requirements.

Close views reveal interfaces, routing, clearances and maintenance access. Findings become questions and constraints for NX, ANSYS and electronics work.

  • Inspect fastening and mounting opportunities
  • Trace nearby hoses, cables and components
  • Document what still requires measurement
