CAE workflow

From engineering question to design decision.

I use this sequence to keep simulation tied to the physical problem rather than treating the contour plot as the final goal.

1

Define question

What failure mode or design risk are we checking?

2

Prepare model

Geometry cleanup, contact assumptions and relevant simplifications.

3

Material & BCs

Assign material, supports, loads or thermal conditions.

4

Mesh

Choose a mesh appropriate to geometry and the evaluation goal.

5

Solve & inspect

Read stress, deformation, safety concepts or temperature fields.

6

Iterate

Use the result to revise geometry or assumptions and re-check.

Structural

Static Structural, stress and deformation evaluation.

Strength

Material-strength and safety-factor concepts in design review.

Thermal

Basic thermal-analysis workflow for temperature-related checks.
