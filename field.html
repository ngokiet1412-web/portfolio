Survey workflow

How I turn a site visit into engineering inputs.

A practical sequence similar to what an engineering team needs before design work begins: capture evidence, classify constraints, then turn them into requirements.

01

Asset identification

Record model, machine family, visible nameplates and system layout so later design decisions are tied to the correct asset.

02

Operating-environment review

Observe vibration, dust/mud, weather exposure, terrain, heat sources and maintenance access.

03

Engine and electrical inspection

Inspect engine-bay routing, connector areas, fuse/relay or diagnostic regions and practical mounting surfaces.

04

Requirement extraction

Translate observations into constraints such as mounting robustness, cable routing, enclosure protection, serviceability and validation checks.

Whole-machine context

Use the full asset view to understand scale, packaging and where subsystems sit relative to one another.

Electrical context

Document existing panels and wiring before deciding how a monitoring device would interface with the machine.

Serviceability

Installation must remain inspectable and maintainable, not only technically functional.
